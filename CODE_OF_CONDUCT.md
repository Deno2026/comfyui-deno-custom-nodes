# Code of Conduct

Deno Custom Nodes is a public project for ComfyUI users, artists, and workflow builders.

Please keep discussion practical, respectful, and focused on the problem being reported.

## Expected Behavior

- Describe bugs with clear reproduction steps.
- Assume other users may have different hardware, runtimes, languages, and experience levels.
- Keep criticism specific to behavior, code, documentation, or workflow results.
- Avoid posting private files, tokens, personal data, or someone else's work without permission.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- discriminatory or sexualized comments
- repeated spam or off-topic promotion
- publishing private information
- intentionally misleading bug reports or malicious instructions

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or delete comments and issues that make the project harder to use safely.

Serious or repeated violations may result in being blocked from participating in this repository.
